# Experiment instructions

Follow root `AGENTS.md` and `PROGRESS.md`. Design begins in Phase 5 after
authorization. Each experiment records hypothesis, intervention, comparator,
duration rationale, primary/secondary outcomes, baseline coverage, confounders,
adherence, recovery review rules, and user approval status. Label proposed versus
active protocols clearly. Do not treat a short personal experiment as proof of
causality or imply statistical significance without an appropriate analysis.
Keep identifying health details in ignored local reports.
